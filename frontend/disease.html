Detectable Osseous Conditions

Osteoarthritis (OA)

Joint space narrowing, subchondral sclerosis, marginal osteophytes, and Kellgren-Lawrence clinical grading.

Osteoporosis / Osteopenia

Cortical thinning, trabecular wash-out, radiodensity proxy scoring, and fragility fracture risk index.

Bone Lesions & Tumors

Focal osteolytic and osteoblastic lucencies, endosteal scalloping, and transition zone margin analysis.

Osteomyelitis (Infection)

Periosteal reaction, patchy medullary radiolucency, and inflammatory cortical erosion screening.
